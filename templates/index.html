How this works. Each pair is long spot on Alpaca and short the same notional on a Hyperliquid perpetual. Price moves cancel; the short collects funding every hour, and the book pays fees on both legs. It leaves a pair when funding turns negative or the basis blows past 2%.

What is real here. The long spot leg is a live order on the Alpaca paper account — filled, sized from the broker's own fill, and reconciled against the broker's positions every cycle. Perp marks, premiums and funding settlements come from Hyperliquid's public feed. Alpaca cannot short crypto and this build holds no perpetuals account, so only the short leg is a ledger position.
