How it trades. One instrument, one rule. Each cycle rebuilds closed 4h bars and asks whether BTC closed above its 200-bar EMA. Above it, the desk holds a core long with an 8-ATR stop underneath; a close more than 2 ATR below that line is the brake, which sells and waits for a close back above the line. No trailing stop and no scalping, because on Alpaca's 0.25%-per-side crypto fee every extra round trip has to be paid for before it can earn. Positions are sized to a 2% risk budget, which puts roughly half of equity into BTC.

What is real. Every entry and exit is a market order on the Alpaca paper account, filled and reconciled against the broker's positions. Equity is Alpaca's own equity; P&L is net of the fees actually charged (0.25% per side, taken in coin on buys). The learner only exists in this account's closed trades — with fewer than 5 trades in a bucket it stays neutral, and it needs 8 before it will refuse a setup. All results remain paper until the account is funded and the build is pointed at it.
